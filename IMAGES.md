# Editorial imagery — 16 October 2026

The catalog hero and category shelves use illustrative scenes under
`public/images/editorial/`, with 480, 960 and 1440 px WebP exports.
`src/lib/editorialImages.ts` explicitly assigns known category slugs. Unknown
categories stay text-only. Both languages use the same subjects. The empty
catalog's eight service areas have matching decorative artwork as well.

Product images, partner images and product structured data remain owned by
the catalog API. Generated scenes never stand in for an actual product.

The new shop hero was generated with the built-in image tool; the remaining
scenes reuse the coordinated editorial series. Original PNGs, WebP exports,
prompts and gallery live in the workspace at
`stuff/03-Materialien/Bildserie-2026-10-16/`, under Shop, Tools, Blog and Leistungen.
